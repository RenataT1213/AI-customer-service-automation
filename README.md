AI Customer Service Automation for E-commerce
Overview
This project presents an end-to-end AI transformation initiative for an e-commerce company selling home furnishing products.

The objective was to identify business processes with the highest automation potential, evaluate AI opportunities, design a pilot implementation, calculate expected ROI, and create a reusable AI assistant for customer service operations.

Selected AI Pilot
Customer Email Support
The selected pilot focuses on automating customer email support through AI-assisted response generation.

Expected outcomes:

Faster response preparation
Reduced manual workload
Improved consistency
Fewer customer service errors
Better customer experience
Success Metrics
Metric	Current	Target
Response Time	10 min	5 min
Monthly Effort	183 h	90 h
Errors	5/month	2/month
Customer Wait Time	24 h	8 h
Human Oversight
All AI-generated responses must be reviewed and approved by a customer service specialist before being sent to customers.

Repository Purpose
This repository was created as a practical AI business transformation case study demonstrating how a small e-commerce company can identify, prioritize, and implement AI-driven process improvements.
