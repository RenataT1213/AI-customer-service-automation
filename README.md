# AI Customer Service Automation for E-commerce

## Overview

This project presents an end-to-end AI transformation initiative for an e-commerce company selling home furnishing products.

The objective was to identify business processes with the highest automation potential, evaluate AI opportunities, design a pilot implementation, calculate expected ROI, and create a reusable AI assistant for customer service operations.

## Project Scope

The project includes:

- Process Audit
- Automation Design
- AI Adoption Plan
- ROI Analysis
- AI Skill Design
- Deep Research Framework

---

## Business Context

**Industry:** E-commerce

**Company Size:** 20 Employees

**Departments Audited:**

- Customer Service
- Marketing
- Administration

## Processes Evaluated

### Customer Email Support

**Current State**

- 50 customer inquiries per day
- Average handling time: 10 minutes
- 183 hours of work per month
- 5 response errors per month

**AI Opportunity**

Generate response drafts using:

- Customer inquiry content
- Internal FAQ
- Historical support responses

**AI Value Ladder Level**

Augment

---

### Marketing Content Creation

**Current State**

- 20 posts per month
- 60 hours of work per month

**AI Opportunity**

Generate:

- Product descriptions
- Social media content
- Marketing copy

**AI Value Ladder Level**

Assist

---

### Invoice Registration

**Current State**

- 100 invoices per month
- 8 hours of work per month

**AI Opportunity**

Automatically extract invoice data and prepare accounting records.

**AI Value Ladder Level**

Automate

## Selected Pilot

### Customer Email Support Automation

The selected pilot process focuses on customer email support due to:

- Highest business impact
- Highest implementation feasibility
- Significant time-saving potential

## AI Adoption Strategy

The implementation approach consists of four phases:

### Phase 1 – Preparation

- FAQ preparation
- Knowledge base creation
- AI usage guidelines

### Phase 2 – Pilot

- Limited-user testing
- Response validation
- User feedback collection

### Phase 3 – Team Rollout

- Full deployment
- User training
- Performance monitoring

### Phase 4 – Continuous Improvement

- AI performance review
- FAQ updates
- Process optimization
