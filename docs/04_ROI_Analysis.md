# ROI Analysis

## Current Process Cost

### Assumptions

- Customer inquiries per day: 50
- Average handling time: 10 minutes
- Monthly workload: 183 hours
- Customer service specialist hourly cost: 40 PLN

### Current Monthly Cost

183 hours × 40 PLN

**7,320 PLN per month**

---

## AI Solution Cost

### Monthly Costs

- AI License: 200 PLN
- Maintenance and Configuration: 300 PLN

### Total Monthly Cost

**500 PLN per month**

---

## Expected Benefits

### Time Savings

Current workload:

**183 hours per month**

Future workload:

**90 hours per month**

Time saved:

183 − 90 = **93 hours per month**

### Financial Savings

93 × 40 PLN

**3,720 PLN per month**

---

## Net Monthly Benefit

3,720 PLN − 500 PLN

**3,220 PLN per month**

---

## ROI Calculation

### Formula

ROI = ((Benefit − Cost) / Cost) × 100

### Calculation

ROI = ((3,720 − 500) / 500) × 100

ROI = 644%

---

## Business Conclusion

The proposed AI solution generates estimated monthly savings of **3,720 PLN** while costing approximately **500 PLN per month**.

The estimated monthly net benefit is **3,220 PLN**.

The projected ROI is **644%**, indicating a highly profitable investment.

Under these assumptions, the investment pays for itself during the first month of operation.

---

## Assumptions

- Customer service specialist cost: 40 PLN/hour
- Inquiry volume remains stable at approximately 50 emails per day
- AI reduces handling time from 10 minutes to 5 minutes
- Human review remains mandatory before sending responses
- AI platform and maintenance cost 500 PLN per month
- Qualitative benefits such as customer satisfaction and faster response times are not included in the calculation
