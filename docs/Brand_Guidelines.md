# Brand Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines define the visual and communication style used in presentations, reports, and AI-related materials for the home furnishing e-commerce company.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Color

**Dark Green**

HEX:

```text
#2F5D50
```

Usage:

- Presentation titles
- Section headers
- Key highlights

### Secondary Color

**Light Beige**

HEX:

```text
#DCC9A9
```

Usage:

- Content backgrounds
- Information boxes
- Text highlights

### Accent Color

**Terracotta**

HEX:

```text
#C76D4A
```

Usage:

- Icons
- Important figures
- Key business metrics

### Text Color

**Graphite**

HEX:

```text
#333333
```

Usage:

- Main content
- Paragraphs
- Descriptions

### Background Color

**Soft Cream**

HEX:

```text
#F7F4EF
```

Usage:

- Default slide and document background

---

## Typography

### Primary Font

**Montserrat SemiBold**

Usage:

- Presentation titles
- Section headers

Recommended Size:

- 28–32 pt

### Secondary Font

**Open Sans Regular**

Usage:

- Main content
- Bullet points
- Descriptions

Recommended Size:

- 16–18 pt

### Supporting Font

**Open Sans Light**

Usage:

- Notes
- References
- Additional information

Recommended Size:

- 11–12 pt

---

## Communication Style

The communication style should be:

- Professional
- Modern
- Friendly
- Business-oriented

Guidelines:

- Use clear language
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon
- Focus on business value
- Use concise paragraphs
- Keep content easy to read

---

## Visual Style

Preferred visual elements:

- Interior design photography
- Product photography
- Natural lighting
- Clean layouts
- Modern line icons
- Rounded graphical elements
- Minimalistic composition

Avoid:

- Overcrowded slides
- Excessive animations
- Complex visual effects
- 3D chart effects

---

## Presentation Layout

### Title Slide

Include:

- Interior image background
- Company logo
- Presentation title
- Subtitle if applicable

### Content Slides

Guidelines:

- One clear message per slide
- Maximum five bullet points
- One supporting visual

### Data Slides

Requirements:

- Use company colors
- Include readable labels
- Use simple charts
- Avoid unnecessary decorative elements

---

## Recommended Applications

These guidelines should be applied to:

- AI project presentations
- Business reports
- Customer service improvement initiatives
- Process automation proposals
- Management presentations
- Internal training materials

---

## Brand Objective

The visual identity should communicate:

- Professionalism
- Reliability
- Innovation
- Simplicity
- Customer focus

The overall experience should reflect a modern company successfully adopting Artificial Intelligence and process automation
