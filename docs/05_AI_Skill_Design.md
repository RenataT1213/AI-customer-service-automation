# AI Skill Design – Customer Email Assistant

## Purpose

The AI Skill supports customer service agents by analyzing incoming customer emails and generating response drafts.

The objective is to reduce response preparation time while maintaining response quality and consistency.

---

## Business Context

The company operates an e-commerce store selling home furnishing products.

Customer inquiries typically relate to:

- Order status
- Delivery
- Product returns
- Complaints
- Product availability

The support team handles approximately 50 customer emails per day.

Many inquiries are repetitive but described differently by customers.

---

## When to Use

The skill should be used whenever a new customer email is received.

Typical use cases:

- Order status inquiries
- Delivery issues
- Return requests
- Complaints
- Product availability questions

---

## Input

The user provides:

- Customer email content
- Order information (if available)
- Company procedures
- FAQ information
- Approved response templates

---

## Processing Steps

### Step 1

Identify the inquiry category.

### Step 2

Review available order information.

### Step 3

Review relevant company procedures and FAQ resources.

### Step 4

Generate a response draft.

### Step 5

Identify missing information required to resolve the case.

### Step 6

Return the result in a predefined format.

### Step 7

Customer service agent reviews and approves the response before sending.

---

## Output Format

### Inquiry Category

One of the following:

- Order Status
- Delivery
- Return
- Complaint
- Product Availability
- Other

### Issue Summary

Provide a short summary of the customer's request.

### Response Draft

Generate a professional response draft for the customer.

### Missing Information

List information required to resolve the request.

If nothing is missing, return:

```text
None
```

---

## Rules and Constraints

The AI must:

- Use only provided information
- Follow company procedures
- Maintain a professional tone
- Respond in clear business language

The AI must not:

- Invent order details
- Invent shipping dates
- Invent refund information
- Make business decisions
- Send messages directly to customers

The final decision always belongs to the customer service specialist.

---

## Testing

Before deployment the skill should be tested using sample customer emails covering:

- Order status questions
- Delivery issues
- Returns
- Complaints
- Product availability requests

Validation criteria:

- Correct categorization
- Accurate summaries
- High-quality response drafts
- Compliance with company procedures

---

## Data Protection

The skill may process only the information provided by authorized employees.

Customer data should be handled according to applicable internal privacy and security policies.

Personal information must not be disclosed to unauthorized individuals.

---

## Expected Benefits

- Faster response preparation
- Consistent communication style
- Reduced error rate
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Better productivity for customer service agents

---

## Human Oversight

Every response generated by the AI must be reviewed and approved by a customer service specialist before being sent to a customer
