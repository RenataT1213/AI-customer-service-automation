# Deep Research Prompt

## Objective

We operate an e-commerce company selling home furnishing products.

The customer service department handles approximately 50 customer emails per day related to:

- Order status
- Deliveries
- Returns
- Complaints

The goal is to determine whether AI should be implemented to support customer service operations and which solution offers the best business value.

Prepare a market analysis and provide a business recommendation.

---

## Research Questions

1. Which AI solutions are most commonly used in e-commerce customer service?

2. What measurable benefits do companies achieve after implementing AI for customer communications?

3. What are the implementation and maintenance costs of available solutions?

4. How do Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Zendesk AI, Intercom AI, and similar platforms compare?

5. What risks are associated with AI-driven customer communication?

6. Which KPIs should be monitored after implementation?

7. What ROI can a medium-sized e-commerce company realistically expect?

---

## Scope

Include:

- E-commerce market
- Customer service AI platforms
- Implementations from 2023–2026
- Europe and North America
- Solutions suitable for small and medium-sized businesses

---

## Decision Criteria

Evaluate solutions using the following priorities:

1. Response quality
2. Reduction of handling time
3. Return on investment (ROI)
4. Ease of implementation
5. Customer data security

---

## Exclusions

Do not analyze:

- Enterprise-only solutions
- Platforms unavailable in Europe
- Multi-million-dollar implementations
- Experimental solutions without proven deployments

---

## Sources

Prioritize:

- Industry reports
- E-commerce case studies
- Vendor documentation
- Independent market analysis
- Business and technology publications

Provide sources for all major findings.

---

## Required Output Structure

1. Executive Summary
2. Market Analysis
3. Solution Comparison Table
4. Implementation Examples
5. Cost Analysis
6. Risk Analysis
7. Expected ROI
8. Final Recommendation
9. Three-Month Pilot Plan

---

## Research Rules

- Separate facts from opinions
- Cite sources
- Highlight conflicting findings
- Explain limitations
- Support recommendations with business rationale
- Focus on actions achievable within 12 months
- Use quantitative data whenever possible
